BudgetBuckets — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

The short version: BudgetBuckets collects no data. It has no internet connection at all. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. BudgetBuckets contains no networking code, no analytics, no advertising frameworks, no crash reporters, and no third-party SDKs. It never transmits anything off your device, and it has no servers to send anything to. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Your messages

BudgetBuckets never reads your text messages. iOS does not allow apps to access Messages, and BudgetBuckets does not try. The only way a bank alert enters the app is when you long-press a specific message and share it into BudgetBuckets through the iOS share sheet. The app sees only the individual messages you explicitly choose to share — nothing else, ever.

Where your data lives

Your budget — income, buckets, transactions, monthly defaults, and archives — is stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is included in your normal device backups (iCloud or computer backups you control), and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing a month snapshot

The optional "📤 Share" feature exports a month as a small file that you can send by AirDrop, Messages, or any method you pick from the share sheet. That file goes only where you send it; the app itself transmits nothing.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions — no contacts, no location, no camera, no notifications, no tracking. It does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because it tracks nothing to begin with.

Children

BudgetBuckets does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated first and the change will be described in the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the BudgetBuckets GitHub page and you'll get an answer there.
