BudgetBuckets — Support

Playful envelope budgeting that stays on your phone.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue on the BudgetBuckets GitHub page — it's free and doesn't need anything installed. Describe what happened and you'll get a reply there.

Quick answers

How do expenses get into the app?
Two ways: type them into your plan, or share a bank alert text. In Messages, press and hold the alert bubble → tap Select → tap the › arrow at the end of the small bar that appears → Share… → BudgetBuckets. (The long-press menu itself has no Share — it's behind Select's › arrow.) A card confirms the amount, and the expense appears in the blob tray next time you open the app. BudgetBuckets never reads Messages itself — it only receives the texts you choose to share.
BudgetBuckets isn't in my share sheet.
In the share sheet, swipe the row of app icons to the end and tap More — BudgetBuckets is in that list. Tap Edit and the green + to pin it near the front for next time.
A blob has a blue ring — what does that mean?
It looks like a duplicate of a transaction you already have (banks sometimes send the same alert twice). Tap the blob and choose whether to trash it or keep it.
How do I rename, add, or delete a bucket?
On the Plan screen, tap a bucket's name to edit it, use the "New bucket…" row to add one, or ✕ to delete. On the Buckets screen, long-press a bucket card for Rename/Delete, or tap the dashed "＋ New bucket" tile. Deleting a bucket that holds transactions asks whether they go back to the tray or into the trash.
What happens at the end of the month?
On the Month summary screen, tap 🪣 Empty the buckets. The month is archived, everything resets to your saved Defaults, and you start fresh.
Where is my data? Is anything uploaded?
Everything is stored on your iPhone only. The app has no internet connection — see the privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes your data, so keep device backups on if it matters to you.
